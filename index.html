Where should I measure?

Use a low fret and the last normally shaped fret before the heel. Width is the full neck width. Thickness is wood only, from the flat reference to the center of the back. Added stations estimate a linear taper; measure them to override.

A radiused one-piece top needs a radius-aware measurement method and is outside this tool's scope.

The method, references & final checks

Pass 1 divides the half-width and thickness in half. Pass 2 uses a quarter of the thickness and one-third of the original first facet. Pass 3 uses one-eighth of the width and the midpoint of the original first facet.

The dashed C curve is an elliptical fairing guide. It does not alter your marks or define a historical neck profile. Check the truss-rod channel, hardware clearance and remaining wood against your actual blank before carving; use calipers and a physical template for final shaping.
