Why these numbers?

The first pass halves the half-width and the neck thickness. The second pass halves the remaining side and uses one-third of the original first facet. The third pass halves the remaining back and uses the midpoint of that original first facet.

This is a repeatable rough-carving layout for a symmetric plain C. It is not an industry-standard definition of every "C" profile, and the smooth green curve is only an elliptical fairing guide.

The side measurements require a flat reference line: either a separate fretboard glue line or the still-flat top of a one-piece neck before it is radiused.

Carving reference: Tornelli Guitars, "How to shape a guitar neck"

Double-check before cutting.

Facet Carver is a layout aid, not a guarantee. Verify every measurement against your blank and intended profile. Check the truss rod channel depth, rod hardware, center thickness, and the thinnest finished area before carving. Leave enough back-wall material that you cannot sand or rasp into the truss rod channel. You are responsible for deciding whether the result is safe and appropriate for your instrument.
